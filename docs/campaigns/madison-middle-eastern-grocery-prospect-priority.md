**Gold standard:** Sahadi's (9.4) / Phoenicia (9.0) Google exemplars; profile `mip-n6x5tx3x` v4
**Headline finding:** 0 of 10 candidates meet all gold-standard gates. 0 of 10 have a verified, complete Google Business Profile. Every gate the leader passes is a gap another candidate can close first — and every gate the leader fails is an opening to pitch the leader.

## How Priority Is Scored

Signals, in order of weight:

1. **Reachability** — verified phone/website (can't pitch a ghost).
2. **`business_seek_priority` / `business_seek_recommended`** — the audit's own triage.
3. **Non-negotiable gate failures** (`website_present`, `canonical_nap`, `hours_present`, `category_positioning`) — the more failures, the sharper the pitch, *provided* the business is reachable and real.
4. **`identity_confidence` + discovery signals** — `INT_MULTISOURCE_IDENTITY` + `INT_ACTIVE_OPERATIONAL_EVIDENCE` = confirmed operating business; `INT_SINGLE_SOURCE` / `INT_LOW_VISIBILITY` = verify before spending outreach effort.
5. **Category fit** — `verified` > `probable` > `insufficient` (adjacent businesses are a second-wave motion, not this campaign's targets).
